## Hi, I'm Elaine

My first exposure to technical projects was back in elementary school robotics class. I remember feeling fustrated when I couldn't figure out the last two steps of building my robot car, while all of the other students were already in the hallway racing theirs. The teacher then showed me how switching the location of the large back wheels and small front wheels made my car much faster than if I had followed the guidebook exactly. My biggest takeaway from this is that many things in the world work the same way: there are often multiple solutions to a problem. If your mind is set to resolving it - experimenting, asking for help, thinking critically - you are bound to find an optimal solution. I plan to take this lesson with me as I finish my academic career and work on real-world challenges, hoping to learn from these experiences so I can giveback to my community!
